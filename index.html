Data · MigrationClickHouse · Debezium · Kafka Connect

Moving a live product onto ClickHouse

I owned the migration of analytics storage off PostgreSQL and MongoDB onto ClickHouse while the product kept running, with a star-schema model built in dbt. Change data capture with Debezium and Kafka Connect keeps the new store in sync.

Before cutover I built a shadow-comparison test harness that diffed old and new results. It caught cross-tenant data-scoping leaks that would have shown one customer another customer's data.
